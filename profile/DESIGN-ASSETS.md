# Design Assets

Um:bruch's logo and visual identity are curated, human-designed assets — not
automatically regenerated. The canonical source lives outside this repo in
`OneDrive/.TOPICS/.UMBRUCH/_assets/logos/` (three approved variants; the
letterhead style is used here as `profile/assets/um-bruch-logo.png`).

Automated profile-health or marketing runs must reuse this file, not design a
new banner or logo from scratch.
